Usage
=====

From the Barcode app, open **Cycle Count**, select a planned or open
count, confirm its location, enter the counted quantities for the linked
lines, and apply or explicitly confirm a zero-difference close.

Cycle Count counts existing stock quants; scanning a product does not
create a quant. If no quant matches, or more than one quant matches the
product and its lot/serial identity, stop and do not retry from Barcode.
Regularize the stock or resolve the product, location, and lot/serial
identity through the normal Inventory flow, then retry once the matching
quant exists and is unambiguous.
